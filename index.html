About us

Person-centred care for South East Melbourne

Melbourne Care Ability supports people with disability to live with dignity, choice, and connection. We work with you, your family, and your support network across South East Melbourne.

Support is built around your goals, your culture, and the way you want to live. You decide what good support looks like. We listen, plan with you, and adjust when life changes.

Participants, families, support coordinators, and plan managers are welcome. We respect linguistic and cultural diversity and keep communication plain and direct.

What choice and control means here

  • You choose who supports you, wherever we can match that preference.
  • Goals are written in your words, not only in service language.
  • Plans can change when your needs, home, or goals change.
  • Family and coordinators are included when you want them involved.

Why choose us

Four commitments in every support relationship

Open a card to read how each commitment works in practice.

Person-Centred Approach Tailored individual care plans built around your goals, routines, and preferences.

We start with what matters to you: how you spend your day, who you want involved, and what independence looks like. Supports are written in plain language and reviewed with you.

Dedicated Local Support Workers Experienced and vetted local staff who know South East Melbourne.

Workers are matched with your preferences in mind, including skills, personality, and language where we can. Screening checks are completed before support starts.

Rapid Response & Onboarding Fast intake and clear service agreements so support can begin smoothly.

After a referral we confirm your details, talk through the right registration groups, and prepare a service agreement you can review before anything starts.

Cultural & Linguistic Diversity Inclusive care suited to Melbourne’s multicultural communities.

We respect culture, faith, family roles, and language. Tell us what respectful support looks like for you, and we will build that into the way support is delivered.

Registration groups we support

Fourteen registration groups, from daily personal activities to specialised disability accommodation. Search by keyword.

Accommodation / Tenancy Assistance

Help to find and keep a home, understand a tenancy, and sort practical housing tasks.

Assist Personal Activities High

High intensity daily personal activities for participants who need skilled support with complex personal care.

Learn more about high intensity personal activities

Assist-Life Stage, Transition

Life stage transition support and mentoring through changes such as leaving school, starting work, or moving home.

Learn more about life stage and transition support

Assist-Personal Activities

Daily personal care and assistance that supports your routine, comfort, and privacy at home.

Learn more about daily personal care

Assist-Travel / Transport

Travel and transport arrangements so you can get to appointments, programs, and community activities.

Learn more about travel and transport support

Clinical and specialised health nursing in the community, delivered by qualified nurses.

Learn more about community nursing care

Daily Tasks / Shared Living

Innovative community participation programs shaped around your interests, connection, and goals.
